AI Digest — hero graphic options

Three directions for the landing-page hero, mocked at real size in both themes. The cyber site's hero is an SVG layer (radial glow + concentric rings + a fading dot grid) behind the headline — so these are vector, not generated raster. Nothing here is published.

What gives the cyber hero its character: a rounded hero card, a soft radial glow in the accent colour, thin concentric rings radiating from the headline, a dot grid that fades out downward, a pill kicker, and a two-tone headline (product name + accent word). The AI site currently has the pill kicker and the two-tone idea but no background layer at all — that's the gap these options fill.

Option A Signal rings

The direct sibling. Same grammar as the cyber hero — glow, concentric rings, fading dot grid — recoloured to the AI violet. Reads as unmistakably part of the same family, which is the point if the two products sit side by side on the hub. Risk: it inherits rather than expresses anything specific about AI.

Option B Layer stack

Expresses the product, not just the family. Six luminous strata — one per theme — fading upward. It says "what moved at each layer of the stack", which is the AI Digest's actual organising idea, and it's the thing the cyber site's rings can't say. Risk: less obviously a sibling of the cyber hero.

Option C Node field

The most "AI-coded" of the three. A sparse field of nodes and edges — models, agents, systems — with a few brighter hubs. It signals a computational subject rather than a security one, which is the differentiation the violet accent is already making. Risk: the closest thing here to a cliché, and the least related to the cyber hero.

My recommendation: B. The layer stack says something true about this product that the cyber hero cannot — the six themes are the stack — so it reads as a deliberate sibling rather than a recolour of the same drawing. If you'd rather the two sites read as a matched pair at a glance, take A: that is literally the cyber hero in a different hue. C is the weakest fit — generic AI imagery, and the least related to the cyber hero.

All three are drafts, not finished art: the bands in B and the edges in C currently cross the subtitle, which I'd tighten once a direction is chosen.
Not in these options: the six theme icons (a set of small marks parallel to the cyber site's sector icons), a logo mark, favicon and an OG share image. Those are the next layer once the hero direction is fixed — the hero sets the visual language they inherit.
