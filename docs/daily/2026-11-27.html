Regulators met in Sydney and agreed a joint line on AI-made abuse imagery

The Global Online Safety Regulators Network met in Sydney this week and endorsed a position statement on AI-generated child sexual abuse material and non-consensual intimate imagery. eSafety published the outcome on 25 November. Germany's Federal Network Agency joined during the meetings. Brazil's National Data Protection Agency became the network's first South American member. It was elected vice-chair alongside the Dutch regulator ATKM. Ireland's Coimisiún na Meán was re-elected chair for 2027. The statement calls for greater accountability from technology companies and safeguards built into AI systems, so models cannot produce abusive material in the first place. It also commits members to coordinated regulatory action across borders. eSafety cited Internet Watch Foundation research that counted 13 AI-generated child abuse videos in 2024 and 3,443 in 2025, a rise of 26,385 per cent.

Why it matters

A position statement is not a rule, and the network says as much by noting that members operate under different national law. What changed is coordination: the member regulators now share a written position on the same class of harm, which shortens the distance between a finding in one jurisdiction and an inquiry in another. For anyone running an image or video model, the operative sentence is the one about guardrails at the point of generation rather than at the point of distribution. Australia's own duty of care, enacted on 25 November, is the domestic hook this statement will be cited against.

Research & evidence 1 story

2

A rotation and one scale let a model read another model's mind

Hazy Research at Stanford published Plato's Key on 25 November, testing whether one language model can use another's internal representations without any retraining. The map is a rotation plus a single global scale, fitted in closed form on fewer than 5,000 shared facts. Across 12 base models from eight families, between 1.5 billion and 27 billion parameters, it reached a median transfer accuracy of 41 per cent. That is 73 per cent of the accuracy from swapping in the recipient model's own state. Chaining rotations across three models kept 99 per cent of a direct map, and a 256-component version kept 74 per cent. A rotated donor state also recovered 18 per cent of a recipient's "dark memories", facts it stores but cannot recall, where no control recovered more than 1.1 per cent.

Why it matters

The interesting claim is not that models share a geometry, which was already known. It is that the shared structure is simple enough to align with one closed-form step, which makes cross-model weight surgery cheaper than anyone assumed. Two consequences follow. Interpretability work no longer has to stay inside one model, and a model's hidden state becomes a portable artefact that can be moved between systems. Neither is a safety finding on its own, but both change what a deployment boundary protects. The work is a lab blog post, not a reviewed paper, and no independent group has reproduced it.

Infrastructure & compute 1 story

3

Cloudflare gave every website a search endpoint that agents can find

Cloudflare launched Search for Agents on 25 November, a toggle that builds an index of a domain and advertises a search endpoint to machines. Enabling it creates an AI Search instance that crawls and indexes the zone, then adds metadata to every page head naming the endpoint, the method and the documentation. An agent calls /.well-known/search?q= and receives Markdown results with relevance scores, not a generated answer. The design argument is that the calling agent already has a model, so a second one summarising on its behalf adds nothing it can check. The service is available on the free plan with limits, and it defers to a domain's existing bot management, AI crawler controls and firewall rules.

Why it matters

Publishing that used to serve readers and crawlers now has to serve callers, and a machine-readable endpoint is a cheaper contract than asking every agent to scrape. The parseable detail is that discovery lives in the page head, so a site can advertise a capability without changing its markup. The commercial reading is consolidation: the index, the endpoint and the crawler policy end up in one place, which is the same leverage play that made the CDN layer sticky. Sites that would rather not answer should check their bot rules before they flip the switch, because the endpoint respects them rather than overriding them.

Nvidia stopped servers leaving for four South-East Asian markets

Nvidia has told its manufacturing partners to stop shipping servers built on its chips to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia, the Financial Times reported on 26 November and Reuters carried the same day. Dell and Supermicro, which package Nvidia processors into servers, told buyers in all four countries that the orders would not ship. The halt follows a US Commerce Department rule that took effect on 19 November. It requires a licence for advanced AI chips going to those four countries and to Brazil, with an exemption for servers used by US-headquartered companies. Server makers said it was unclear whether the pause was temporary. Nvidia booked US$104 billion of revenue through its Singapore subsidiary in 2025, up 72 per cent on the year before.

Why it matters

These four countries are the region's main data centre markets, and they sit directly upstream of Australian capacity. The rule reaches a company by where its servers land, not by where the buyer is registered, so the licensing queue now sits inside the delivery schedule of nearly every campus in the region. A three-month licence allowance is running down while the guidance on how to apply is still thin, which is how a supply chain stalls without an order stopping it. Australian builders are outside the listed countries, but they buy in the same market and inherit the same lead times.

5

The chip rules and anti-distillation defences are bending Chinese model plans

The Chinese developer Z.AI, also known as Zhipu AI, has fallen behind on its next flagship model. Rival Moonshot AI expects the interval between major releases to double, from about six months to a year or more. Reuters reported both on 26 November, citing people familiar with the work. Z.AI had planned to release GLM-5.5, a model designed with roughly three trillion parameters, this month. It has not shipped, and more effective safeguards against distillation were part of the delay. The new US computing restrictions push the launch into the first quarter of 2027 or later. Moonshot's estimate assumes it loses access to overseas computing. Z.AI released GLM-5.4 three days earlier, a 750-billion-parameter design it says matches models two to four times its size.

Why it matters

Distillation is how Chinese labs have closed the gap while paying less, and it is the one cost advantage export controls never touched. If the safeguards bite, the penalty is not a halt but a lengthening cycle, which compounds badly for labs that built their reputation on a release every six months. The vendors' own response is the tell here: Z.AI shipped a smaller model and claimed parity by design rather than scale. The claim is a vendor claim and unconfirmed until independent evaluation, but it is the strategy a constrained lab has to run. Watch whether the next flagship arrives in the first quarter or quietly gains a year.

How far the risk, obligation or capability position moves. Impact is not harm: half of what this digest carries — a model release, a research result, a funding round — is not a harm at all. The band is driven by precedent, blast radius, reversibility, structural change and sector criticality, not by a severity dictionary borrowed from cyber incidents.

Three rules that shape what appears

Freshness is a state-change test, not a date test. An item qualifies when something moved — a model shipped, a rule commenced, a vulnerability was exploited, a subpoena was served, a paper published. The window is 48 hours, 72 across a weekend bridge, and it is stated on every edition. Anything older appears in the executive summary as context, never as an entry.

An empty theme stays empty. Where a theme has no qualifying item it is listed in the coverage table with the count it earned and rendered as no section at all — never as a zero bar and never padded with stale material.

Suppression. A story that ran in either of the previous two editions is not repeated unless the day adds a fact, and the new fact must be what the entry is about.
